Battle one-shots used by the game.

These clips were cut from Pixabay CDN previews that were embedded in the
saved search pages supplied with the task, plus the local drums file which
was auditioned and not used. Pixabay lists these sound effects as
royalty-free with no attribution required. The saved pages also named about
forty other titles (tommy gun, infantry, battle cries, war drums, trailer
music). Those files were not in the upload and were not downloaded.

Hooli's tommy burst is the first three cracks of audio_8fdf97e706.mp3
(the dry close-gun preview on the supplied tommy-gun page), trimmed to
weapons/tommy/tommy_burst.wav. Cracks sit at about 18ms, 108ms, and 198ms.
The other four previews on that page are drums, a whoosh, a bass tone, and
a long bed. Those stay out of the gun.

Used, after trim, fade, a light high-pass, and peak leveling:
- audio_8fdf97e706.mp3 — dry close cracks → weapons/rifle/rifle_shot_01–05
- audio_0c72fb8780.mp3 — short low-mid hits → heavy_shot_01, body_impact_01–03, heavy_impact_01, explosion_small_02
- audio_357038f64c.mp3 — thin high noise bursts → metal_impact_01–04, distant_shot_01–04
- audio_14558e2f5d.mp3 — airy swells → movement/whoosh_01–02
- audio_9df6700a8b.mp3 — one decaying blast → explosion_small_01, explosion_medium_01, explosion_large_01
  (three lengths of the same recording, not three different explosions)

- audio_d45c189487.mp3, audio_dd84218105.mp3, audio_e26f963cd2.mp3 — tonal bass / drum music
