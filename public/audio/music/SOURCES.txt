battle_drums.flac

Source recording: tatamusic — battle warrior fighting drums (Pixabay 478210),
the MP3 supplied with the project. Stereo, about 125 BPM.

The supplied file peaks above full scale and spends its last five seconds
falling into silence, so a raw loop would click and then go quiet.
The game copy keeps the drum body from the opening hit (0.05s) through 37.1s,
wraps the join with a 140ms equal-power crossfade, and scales the peak to
0.89. No extra compression. One looping voice on the music bus.
